# Privacy Policy – Casa Escencia
Last updated: February 10, 2026
At Casa Escencia, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, create an account, or make a purchase. By using our site, you agree to the terms described below.If you do not agree with this policy, we recommend that you do not use our website.
## 1. Information We Collect
We collect personal information to process your orders, provide customer support, and ensure the proper functioning of our online store.
### 1.1. Information You Provide Directly
When placing an order or creating an account, we may request the following information:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Billing address
- Shipping address
- Order details
- Payment information (processed securely through third-party gateways)
Note: Casa Escencia does not store credit or debit card numbers.
### 1.2. Information Collected Automatically
Our website automatically collects certain data, including:
- IP address
- Browser type
- Device information
- Pages visited
- Session duration
- Cookies and tracking tools
- Referring URLs
This information helps us optimize website performance and user experience.
## 2. How We Use Your Information
Personal information is used to:
- Process payments and orders
- Deliver purchased items
- Provide customer support
- Manage customer accounts
- Send notifications about your order
- Improve site performance and functionality
- Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized activity
We never sell your personal information.
## 3. WooCommerce Account & Checkout Settings
### 3.1. Guest Checkout
Customers may complete purchases without creating an account.
### 3.2. Account Creation
Accounts may be created:
- During checkout
- On the My Account page
WooCommerce may automatically generate usernames, or customers may create their own.
### 3.3. Password Access
New users receive a secure link to set their password.Casa Escencia does not know or store your password.

We use cookies to:
- Enable essential store functions

## 7. Sharing Personal Information
We only share your data when necessary for operations.
### 7.1. Shipping Providers
We use a national courier service for deliveries within El Salvador.International orders may involve carriers like DHL, FedEx, or similar services.
Information shared may include:
- Name
- Address
- Phone number
### 7.2. Payment Processors
Secure payment gateways process your payment information.Casa Escencia does not store card details.
### 7.3. Technology & Security Tools
We may use third-party services to:
- Detect fraud
- Improve performance
- Maintain website stability
These providers are not authorized to use your data for their own purposes.
## 8. Information Security
We take appropriate measures to protect your personal information, including:
- SSL encrypted browsing
- Secure payment processing
- Restricted internal access
- Updated security plugins
- Regular system monitoring
However, no online platform is completely risk-free.
## 9. Shipping & Logistics (Privacy Considerations)
To fulfill your order, we share only essential data with shipping partners:
- Name
- Address

- Casa Escencia is not responsible for delays caused by shipping companies.
- Any international duties or taxes are the customer’s responsibility.
